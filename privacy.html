Your home stays yours.

Privacy policy · Effective 12 October 2026 · Developer: Daniel Hall

Direct connection

Widget Studio connects directly to the Home Assistant server you choose. We do not operate an app cloud service that receives your device readings or access token. Your Home Assistant provider may process requests according to its own policies.

Information on your device

The app stores your server address, device readings, favourites, room labels and widget layouts in its local shared container so the app and widgets can work together. Your access and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain using device-only protection. The app and widget extension may access these credentials to authenticate to your selected server. It is not stored in a widget URL, logged, or transmitted to the developer.

No tracking

The app includes no ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or in-app purchases. We do not sell your data. Apple may process diagnostics and TestFlight feedback according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Widget visibility

Widgets may display selected home readings on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Your iOS privacy settings affect visibility. Device actions require authentication. Locks are monitoring only; scenes, scripts and covers open the app for confirmation.

Deleting information

Open Settings → Disconnect and erase local data to remove the stored token, readings and layouts. To revoke the server credential, remove the Widget Studio refresh token (or manually created long-lived token) in your Home Assistant profile → Security. Sign-in uses the system browser directly with your selected Home Assistant server. The support website publishes only the approved app callback; it does not receive your authorisation code or credentials. Local discovery reads Home Assistant announcements on your network only when you choose to search. Removing the app alone may not remove Keychain records, so disconnect first if you want to erase the credential.

Support and this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information under its privacy statement. If you voluntarily report an issue through GitHub, that report may be public. Do not include credentials or private home details. Contact the developer through the support issue tracker.

Policy updates

This policy describes version 1 of the app. Changes to data handling will be reflected here before release.

Widget Studio is independent of Home Assistant, the Open Home Foundation and Nabu Casa.
