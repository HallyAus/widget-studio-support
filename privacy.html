Your home stays yours.

Privacy policy · Updated 15 October 2026 · Developer: Daniel Hall
Widget Studio: Home Assistant for iPhone and Android

Direct Home Assistant connection

The app connects directly to the Home Assistant server you choose. We do not operate a developer cloud, account system or relay receiving your credentials, readings or camera images. Your chosen server, hosting and remote-access providers handle requests under their own practices.

Sign-in opens your selected Home Assistant server in the system browser; the app does not read your password. A manually created access token is another option. Credentials authenticate only to your selected, validated origin. Remote servers require HTTPS. If you choose a supported private-network HTTP address, that local connection is not encrypted in transit; use a trusted network or HTTPS.

Information on your device

Saved information can include server addresses, source identifiers/names, readings/history, calendars, to-do items, weather, presence, room labels, favourites, dashboard paths, widget layouts, connection observations and action receipts. This lets widgets retain saved readings when a connection fails. Camera stills require explicit consent and private, size-limited storage; they are not uploaded to the developer.

On iPhone, credentials use the device-only iOS Keychain and the app and widgets share a private app container. On Android, credentials, configuration, readings, diagnostic events and consented stills use private files encrypted with an Android Keystore key, excluded from operating-system backup and device transfer. Camera permission is not requested: optional images come from the Home Assistant source you select.

No tracking

There are no advertising, analytics or automatic crash-upload SDKs, tracking, subscriptions or in-app purchases. We do not sell data. Apple, Google and your device vendor may provide their own store, crash and beta-feedback services under their settings and policies.

Optional diagnostic support reports

Settings → Diagnostics (Help & Diagnostics on iPhone) lets you prepare a report offline, choose whether to include recent logs, preview the exact text, and share through another app. Nothing is sent automatically. Reports include app version/build and operating-system information; Android also includes device manufacturer/model and aggregate configuration counts. An optional description is included only in the report you prepare.

Automatic events contain fixed operation/error categories, numeric status codes, timings and limited technical summaries. They exclude credentials, addresses, entity names, readings, images and command parameters. Known credentials, links and common secret patterns are removed from entered descriptions, but redaction cannot find every kind of private information. Review all text before sharing.

Logs are bounded to 72 hours and 1,000 events, pruned when accessed or written; operating-system suspension can leave periods unobserved. On Android they are encrypted and excluded from backup. Clear diagnostic logs removes the saved local journal without deleting your home setup; later activity can create new events. A complete crash or outage history is not promised.

If you email a report to support@agenticconsciousness.com.au, we receive it and your correspondence through Microsoft 365. This can link it to your email identity. We use it to investigate and respond to your request and improve the relevant functionality, with no advertising or tracking. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations. Request deletion at that address. Other destinations you choose have their own policies.

Configuration files and recipes

Export/import is user initiated, with a preview before applying or sharing. Portable files exclude credentials, server addresses, readings, camera images and diagnostic events. Configuration backups can contain entity identifiers, custom labels and layout choices that reveal your setup; review them before sharing. Recipes map to local sources you choose. Imported camera layouts require new consent. The system share chooser grants temporary access to the selected file.

Widget visibility and actions

Selected readings may be visible to anyone who can see your Home Screen. Lock Screen and privacy behavior vary by platform/device. Sensitive camera content is hidden on untrusted or protected widget surfaces. Actions require a fresh state check and explicit intent. They are never queued offline or automatically retried. Locks are read-only; scenes, scripts and covers require app confirmation. Additional homes are read-only for actions.

Android appliance sessions are user-started, display-only ongoing notifications with an expiry and Stop control. The operating system controls background scheduling; foreground polling does not guarantee a widget refresh interval.

Optional personal referrals

Settings includes optional Tesla, Starlink and OVO Energy links. Daniel may receive a reward if you use one and qualify. Links are bundled, never preloaded or opened automatically, and contain no household data or app-generated user identifier. We do not record taps. Providers open outside the app and process visits/purchases under their own policies. Current terms and regional eligibility apply. Referral use does not affect app features.

Removing your information

Disconnect a home in Settings to remove its credentials, saved readings and camera files. On Android, disconnected layouts remain available for explicit source repair; delete unwanted layouts separately and use Clear diagnostic logs to remove the journal. On iPhone, Disconnect and erase local data also removes the local setup and diagnostics. Export a configuration first if you want to keep it.

Revoke the server credential in Home Assistant → Profile → Security. There is no separate Widget Studio account to delete. Uninstalling Android removes private app data; iOS Keychain records can survive removal, so disconnect first to erase credentials. Manage already shared copies with their recipients.

Support website

This site uses GitHub Pages, which may process standard requests under GitHub's privacy statement. Its approved callback declaration does not receive authorisation codes or credentials. Support, privacy and referral websites open only when you choose. A public issue is visible to others: exclude tokens, private addresses and household details.

Changes and contact

We update this policy before a release changes these practices. Contact support@agenticconsciousness.com.au for privacy questions or support-data deletion.
