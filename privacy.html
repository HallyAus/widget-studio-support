Your home stays yours.

Privacy policy · Effective 13 October 2026 · Developer: Daniel Hall

Direct connection

Widget Studio connects directly to the Home Assistant server you choose. We do not operate an app cloud service that receives your device readings or access token. Your Home Assistant provider may process requests according to its own policies.

Information on your device

The app stores your server address, device readings, saved history, calendars, to-do items, weather and presence states, saved captions, dashboard paths, source suggestions, connection observations, private action receipts, favourites, room labels and widget layouts in its local shared container so the app and widgets can work together. Your access and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain using device-only protection. The app and widget extension may access these credentials to authenticate to your selected server. It is not stored in a widget URL, logged, or transmitted to the developer.

No tracking

The app includes no ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or in-app purchases. We do not sell your data. Apple may process diagnostics and TestFlight feedback according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Optional diagnostic support reports

From build 8, Settings → Help & Diagnostics lets you describe a problem, choose whether to include local diagnostic logs, preview the complete report and share it through another app. Nothing is uploaded automatically and no developer account is needed. The report includes the app/build and iOS versions, whether it came from a simulator, your description, and a new report reference. Included logs contain technical error categories, numeric status codes, app/widget source, local/remote route category, timings, saved-data age and summary performance measurements. Automatic logs exclude HA tokens, addresses, entity names, readings and device-control parameters. Known device credentials, URLs and common secret patterns are removed from your description. This cannot identify every kind of private information; review the complete report before sharing.

Logs are stored in the device shared container, excluded from backup, and bounded to 72 hours and 1,000 events. Old records are pruned when the app reads or writes the journal; iOS suspension can leave periods unobserved. Clear diagnostic logs removes the local timeline without removing layouts. Disconnect also clears diagnostics. No complete outage or crash record is promised. Apple manages its own crash-reporting and TestFlight feedback services.

If you choose to email a report to support@agenticconsciousness.com.au, we receive the report and your email correspondence through Microsoft 365. This can link the technical report to your email identity. We use it only to investigate and respond to your support request and improve the relevant app functionality, with no advertising or tracking. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion at the support address. Sharing to a different destination is your choice and is governed by that service.

Widget visibility

Widgets may display selected home readings on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Your iOS privacy settings affect visibility. Device actions require authentication. Locks are monitoring only; scenes, scripts and covers open the app for confirmation.

Deleting information

Open Settings → Disconnect and erase local data to remove the stored token, readings, layouts and local diagnostic logs. To revoke the server credential, remove the Widget Studio refresh token (or manually created long-lived token) in your Home Assistant profile → Security. Sign-in uses the system browser directly with your selected Home Assistant server. The support website publishes only the approved app callback; it does not receive your authorisation code or credentials. Local discovery reads Home Assistant announcements on your network only when you choose to search. Removing the app alone may not remove Keychain records, so disconnect first if you want to erase the credential.

Support and this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information under its privacy statement. If you voluntarily report an issue through GitHub, that report may be public. Do not include credentials or private home details. For private support, email support@agenticconsciousness.com.au. Support correspondence is handled through Microsoft 365 and is used to respond to your request. Include only the information needed for support. The app does not automatically send diagnostic files or home data. You may also use the public issue tracker.

Policy updates

This policy describes version 1 of the app. Changes to data handling will be reflected here before release.

Widget Studio is independent of Home Assistant, the Open Home Foundation and Nabu Casa.
