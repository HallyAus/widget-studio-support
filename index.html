From your home to your Home Screen

  1. Connect your homeEnter your server address and choose Sign in with Home Assistant. The system browser opens your own server; Widget Studio does not read your password.
  2. Choose and save a layoutReview suggested readings or choose your own sources. Check names, units and the preview, then give the layout a recognisable name.
  3. Place the saved widgetAdd Widget Studio through your device’s widget controls, then select the exact layout you saved. Check its real readings on your Home Screen.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Hold the Home Screen and open Edit → Add Widget.
  2. Search for Widget Studio and choose a supported size.
  3. Hold the placed widget, choose Edit Widget and select your saved layout.

For Lock Screen widgets, hold the Lock Screen → Customise → Lock Screen, then choose a supported Widget Studio widget.

Android

  1. Use Add to Home on a saved layout and confirm your launcher’s pin dialog.
  2. Or hold the Home Screen → Widgets → Widget Studio.
  3. Choose your saved layout and resize it using your launcher’s controls.

Available sizes and Lock Screen support depend on your launcher, device and Android version.

Apple Watch: the iPhone app supports selected readings and supported complications. Enable Watch sharing in the app and review which readings you want to send. Delivery and refresh timing are controlled by the devices.

Get a clear answer when something stops working

My widget shows an old reading

Check the reading’s age and connection information, then open the app and refresh. The app can keep a saved reading while Home Assistant or your connection is unavailable.

iOS and Android decide when background widgets can refresh. The app’s foreground polling setting does not set a background widget schedule. A refresh request is not a guarantee that the system will redraw the widget immediately.

It works at home, but not away

A local address normally works only on your home network. Remote access needs your own reachable HTTPS Home Assistant address or an existing VPN connection. Widget Studio does not provide a cloud relay or set up a VPN.

Review Homes & connections and the remote-access guide. Authenticate each configured address as instructed. Keep certificate checks enabled.

Sign-in or connection checks fail

Check the server address and whether Home Assistant opens normally on this device. If credentials have expired or been revoked, sign in again through the app’s connection settings.

For a local connection, review Local Network permission on iPhone or iPad. A timeout alone does not prove that permission is denied. Use Check my connection or Diagnostics to investigate further.

A reading is missing or its source was renamed

Open the affected layout and explicitly replace or remove the missing source. Review source names and units before saving. The app does not invent a replacement reading.

Bin dates, charts or calendars are missing

These features depend on your configured Home Assistant sources. Charts require available numeric history from Recorder. Bins need a supported date sensor, Waste Collection Schedule source or calendar, with the appropriate waste-type filter.

Widget Studio does not supply council collection dates. Unknown dates and unavailable history should remain distinguishable from saved data.

Can a widget run a command while I’m offline?

Household commands require an explicit action and a fresh state check. They are not queued offline or automatically replayed later. Locks are read only; scenes, scripts and covers require confirmation in the app.

Where are the latest release notes and known limitations?

Read the current release notes on the App Store or Google Play. Background refresh timing, available widget sizes and some system controls depend on your platform.

If a problem remains, include your app version, device model and a description of what happened in an optional support report. Physical widget behaviour cannot be confirmed from an in-app preview alone.

Help with your setup

Open Settings and choose Report a problem, Help & Diagnostics or Diagnostics, depending on your app version. Describe the issue, choose whether to include the local log, and review the exact report before sharing.

Only information you choose to send to support is sent to us. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Reports can be prepared while offline.

Email support@agenticconsciousness.com.au, with “Widget Studio” in the subject. For a public feature request, use the issue page.

Keep access tokens, passwords, private addresses, household details and unredacted screenshots out of public issues. Review private reports before sending, too.
